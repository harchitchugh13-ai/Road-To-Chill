About

One new game, every day

publishes an original browser game every day: puzzles, number games, word games and quick learning challenges. Every game is designed in-house, plays instantly on a phone or computer, and needs no download or sign-up.

Why we make them

Short games are a good way to practise something real, like times tables, state capitals or logical thinking, without it feeling like homework. Each game takes a few minutes and teaches or sharpens one thing.

How the site is funded

The games are free. The site is supported by advertising shown around the games, never inside them.
